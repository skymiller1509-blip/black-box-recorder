Honest limits: the recorder logs what the agent was given and what it decided. The reasoning steps are written out by the model itself, not read from inside SERV Reasoning. A what-if is a fresh SERV call with changed inputs, not a rewind of the original run. Nothing is stored on the server. Sample data only.
